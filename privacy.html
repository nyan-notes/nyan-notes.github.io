Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026

The short version: Nyan Notes doesn't collect anything. Your notes stay on your PC.

The app

  • There's no account, no sign-in and no cloud.
  • The app doesn't send your notes, your file names or anything about how you use it to anyone. It has no analytics and no tracking.
  • It doesn't check for updates or contact any server on its own.
  • Pictures from the web that a note points to (an https:// image) are loaded from their website, like in any markdown viewer. Links you click open in your own browser.

What it keeps on your PC

  • Its settings, your recent files and where you were in each note, in %APPDATA%\NyanNotes.
  • Older versions of your notes (a copy of a file before a save replaces it, kept for up to 60 days), in %LOCALAPPDATA%\NyanNotes\history.
  • Uninstalling removes the app. To remove these too, delete those two folders.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. The company hosting it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.
